01 Information we see

All purchases are processed through Etsy's checkout, not a website we operate ourselves. When you buy from FUERTE MIND on Etsy, Etsy shares with us the order details needed to fulfill it:

  • Name and shipping address
  • Order contents, size/color selections, and order total
  • Email address, for order-related communication through Etsy's messaging system

We do not collect payment card details — Etsy and its payment processors handle that directly and never share full card numbers with us.

02 How it's used

We use order information only to:

  • Send your order to Printify for printing and shipping
  • Respond to questions about an order
  • Track basic sales and demand for our own designs

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising outside of the platforms (Etsy, TikTok) where you already interact with us.

03 Third parties involved

Because FUERTE MIND runs on third-party platforms rather than our own storefront, several companies process parts of your data independently, under their own privacy policies:

  • Etsy — marketplace, checkout, payments, and buyer/seller messaging
  • Printify — order fulfillment, printing, and shipping of physical products
  • TikTok — if you follow, comment on, or message us on TikTok, that interaction is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy

We encourage you to review each platform's policy for details on how they specifically handle your data.

04 Cookies & tracking

FUERTE MIND does not operate its own website with tracking cookies. Any cookies or analytics you encounter while browsing our Etsy shop or TikTok profile belong to those platforms, not to us.

05 Data retention

We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, warranty, and customer-service purposes, and no longer than the platforms we use (Etsy, Printify) retain the same records on their end.

06 Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what order information we hold about you, or to delete it once an order is complete and no longer needed for legal or accounting purposes. Reach out using the contact info below. For data held directly by Etsy, Printify, or TikTok, you may also need to contact them directly.

07 Children's privacy

FUERTE MIND's products and content are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

08 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our shop or social channels after a change means you accept the update.

09 Contact

Questions about this policy or a request about your data? Reach us through our Etsy shop messages, or through our TikTok profile.

This is a plain-language policy for a small, independent shop. If you need this reviewed for a specific legal jurisdiction, a lawyer familiar with e-commerce and platforms like Etsy and TikTok can tailor it further.
